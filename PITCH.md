# 🏛️ PANCHAYAT — Hackathon Pitch Script
### 2-3 Minute Checkpoint Presentation

---

## 🎯 Opening — The Hook (20 seconds)

> "What if you could **run for office** against AI politicians who think, strategize, and fight back — using real Indian political data?"
>
> **Panchayat** is an AI-powered post-truth political simulator where the player competes against 4 AI candidates in a Panchayat election. Every AI opponent has a distinct ideology, reacts to your policies in real-time using Google Gemini, and fights to win over 5 voter demographics.

---

## 🧠 The Problem (20 seconds)

> Political literacy in India is low. People vote on vibes, not policy.
>
> We asked: **What if we made politics a game?** A game where you *feel* the consequences of policy decisions — where raising MSP makes farmers love you but tanks your business vote, where promising free healthcare sounds great until the AI opponent exposes your funding gap.
>
> **Panchayat makes you live the tradeoffs that real politicians face.**

---

## 🏗️ What We Built (60 seconds)

> Our architecture has 3 layers:

### 1. Data Intelligence Layer ✅ (Complete)
> - **4 AI candidate personas** — Traditionalist, Techno-Populist, Socialist, Libertarian — each with a unique Gemini system prompt, 8-axis ideology vector, and complete backstory
> - **5 voter demographics** — Farmers (30%), Urban Youth (25%), Small Business (20%), Govt Employees (15%), Rural Women (10%) — with sentiment engines that calculate real-time happiness shifts
> - **20 curated real Indian political scenarios** — Farm Bills, Demonetization, GST, NEET paper leaks — pre-analyzed for all 4 candidates
> - **Mathematical scoring engine** — Cosine similarity for ideology matching, softmax-weighted voting for election simulation
> - **694 validation tests, all passing**

### 2. AI Reasoning Engine ✅ (Working Prototype)
> - **LangGraph ReAct agents** — Each AI candidate is a separate agentic loop powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash
> - **Tool-augmented reasoning** — Before responding, each AI searches our scenario database, checks voter sentiment, and grounds its reaction in real data
> - **Example**: When the player announces "Increase MSP by 50%", the Traditionalist candidate searches Farm Bills 2020 context, checks farmer sentiment (42/100), and responds: *"As our scriptures teach, Annadāta Sukhibhava... but this is vikas ka bhram"*

### 3. Real-Time Multiplayer Backend ✅ (SpacetimeDB)
> - **SpacetimeDB module** — Zero-sum voter share redistribution
> - **5×5 voter-candidate matrix** — Every vote gained by one candidate is lost by another
> - **Instant sync** — All players see state changes in real-time via WebSocket subscriptions
